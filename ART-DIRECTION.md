# Vivaldi art direction

Four new wide triptych texture atlases were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and copied into public/art/. Each third becomes a separate movement painting through UV coordinates. No API-key CLI fallback was used.

- vivaldi-spring.png: Venetian Baroque-style spring paintings: birds and a brook; a resting shepherd; a pastoral dance.
- vivaldi-summer.png: summer heat; a landscape awaiting a storm; wind, rain and lightning.
- vivaldi-autumn.png: grape harvest still life; a quiet feast table after the celebration; golden autumn woodland.
- vivaldi-winter.png: bare trees in snow; a violin beside a fireplace; a frozen Venetian waterway.

The shared prompt specifies three equal complete compositions, early eighteenth-century Venetian oil painting, old-master detail and atmospheric depth, without text, frames or watermarks. Autumn and winter were generated as landscapes and still life without human figures.

Two historical public-domain portraits (vivaldi-portrait.jpg and vivaldi-engraving.jpg) are sourced and attributed in CREDITS.md. The new seasonal illustrations are not historical Vivaldi works. The interface retains the existing subtle parchment texture, Cinzel lettering and Cormorant Garamond display typography.

The Blender model enlarges the hall to 1,408 m², assigns a distinct silk-wall pigment to each season, frames twelve separate movement illustrations, and provides additional listening desks and a shop. The shared layout JSON keeps the geometry, direct arrivals and collision system aligned.
