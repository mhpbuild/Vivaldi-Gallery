# Keep it peaceful ✌🏻

This project exists to learn and enjoy music. Everyone participating in issues, discussions, reviews and contributions should:

- Treat others with respect, including artists, rights holders and people with different opinions.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work.
- Respect privacy and copyright; do not post personal information without permission.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, insults and unwanted promotion.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation to keep the project welcoming. Raise conduct concerns privately through the reporting route in SECURITY.md; do not publish another person's private details.

Bring curiosity, patience and good intentions. Peace ✌🏻
